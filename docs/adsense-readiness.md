# AdSense preparation

Scope: Jornal do Cinema (.com.br), not the .pt sites or the Hub backend.

## Implemented

- Publisher verification via meta tag and ads.txt for pub-7037572686437222.
- No advertising scripts, analytics or application cookies/local storage.
- Server-rendered articles and navigation, working search and real 404/503 responses.
- Contact, about, editorial, privacy and cookie pages using the supplied public email and CNPJ. No invented legal name or staff biographies.
- Preservation of original bylines, image credits and source links.
- Only explicitly cinema-categorized or verified cinema-source articles are displayed; unrelated records remain untouched in the Hub.
- Syndicated and thin article pages are noindex and omitted from sitemap. This is search hygiene, NOT a workaround for replicated-content eligibility.

## Still required before requesting review

- Expand relevant cinema coverage. The inspected feed had 25 records but only two with confirmed cinema source URLs.
- Publish genuine original editorial work or substantial independently edited value. Owning the source and preserving credits alone do not establish AdSense eligibility.
- Confirm the actual publisher identity and review institutional/privacy disclosures. The user chose not to supply a legal name yet; the public email and CNPJ are not a substitute for all applicable identification requirements.
- Check image republication rights and source permissions.
- Configure Google's certified consent solution where required before activating ads; update disclosures for the actual advertising setup.
- Add jornaldocinema.com.br in the AdSense account and check account-specific requirements. Do not request review merely because ads.txt is present.

Google decides approval. No approval or revenue promise is made.
